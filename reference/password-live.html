The Volturia NFC Chip works like magic: simply place your smartphone over the chip to instantly view the Digital Garment Passport for your piece. The first time you use it, you will be asked if you want to permanently register the garment on the Blockchain. Once registered, the garment becomes uniquely yours, verifiable at any time, and linked to exclusive contents and promotions accessible only to its owner.

The Volturia Virtual Try-On transforms the way you shop. At checkout, you can see exactly how the garments you’ve selected will fit your body. Simply create a personalized avatar with your exact measurements (no personal photos required) and try the pieces on digitally. This ensures a perfect fit, eliminates returns, and reduces logistical waste, keeping both the planet and our customers happy!
